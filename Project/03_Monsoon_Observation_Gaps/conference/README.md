## 🖼️ Poster

**File:** `poster/poster_final.pdf`

**File:** `poster/poster_source.pptx`

**Format:** 90 cm × 120 cm (standard conference poster)

**Key Sections:**
- Introduction & Research Question
- Study Area
- Methodology
- Key Results (with figures)
- Discussion
- Recommendations
- Limitations
- References

**Key Findings Highlighted:**
- 126 acquisition opportunities analyzed
- Only 47 (37.30%) valid at 50% threshold
- Maximum gap: 55 days (June 5 – July 30, 2023)
- 90% threshold increased gap to 90 days
- 58 acquisitions (46%) had zero clear cropland pixels
