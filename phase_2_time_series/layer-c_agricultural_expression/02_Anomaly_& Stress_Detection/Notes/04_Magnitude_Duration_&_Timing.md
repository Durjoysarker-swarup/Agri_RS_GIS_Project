# 4.4 — Magnitude, Duration & Timing

## Part 1 — Anomalies Have Multiple Characteristics

### Magnitude

How different is the observation from the expected baseline?

**Example**

- Expected NDVI → 0.78
- Observed NDVI → 0.62
- Magnitude → -0.16

A larger magnitude means the vegetation differs more from what was expected.

### Duration

How long an anomaly persists.

**Example:** Imagine two anomalies.

**Anomaly A**

| Week | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|------|---|---|---|---|---|
| NDVI | 0 | 0 | ↓ | 0 | 0 |

Only one week.

**Anomaly B**

| Week | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|------|---|---|---|---|---|
| NDVI | ↓ | ↓ | ↓ | ↓ | ↓ |

Five weeks.

Very different duration.

**Why Duration Matters**

Many biological stresses develop over time.

**Examples**
- prolonged drought,
- nutrient deficiency,
- waterlogging.

Short-lived disturbances may simply reflect
- passing clouds,
- temporary measurement errors,
- brief weather events.

Persistence often carries more biological significance than a single extreme value.

### Timing

The same anomaly can have completely different consequences depending on when it occurs.

Imagine a -0.15 NDVI anomaly.

Does it matter? → The answer depends on the growth stage.

**During Emergence**

Possible consequences
- poor establishment,
- reduced plant population,
- uneven canopy.

Early damage may affect the entire season.

**During Tillering**

Possible consequences
- fewer tillers,
- lower canopy development,
- slower biomass accumulation.

**During Heading**

Possible consequences
- reduced flowering success,
- lower grain number,
- reduced yield potential.

**During Grain Filling**

Possible consequences
- smaller grains,
- reduced grain weight,
- lower final yield.

**During Senescence**

A negative anomaly may simply indicate normal aging, not stress.

Every anomaly should be described using three questions.

- Magnitude
- Duration
- Timing

Together they provide a much richer description than magnitude alone.

### Severity

Emphasize that severity is not directly measured. It is inferred by combining:

**Magnitude + Duration + Timing → Potential Severity**

## Part 2 — Describing an Anomaly

Instead of writing

*NDVI decreased.*

Write something like

| Characteristic | Description |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Direction | Negative |
| Magnitude | Moderate |
| Duration | Four weeks |
| Timing | Heading stage |
| Pattern | Gradual persistent anomaly |

This is still description.

Interpretation comes later.
