# 📚 Literature

This folder documents my journey of learning from research papers in **Agriculture, Remote Sensing, GIS, AI, and Data Science**.

The purpose is **not** to summarize papers. Instead, I capture the ideas, methods, and lessons that improve my understanding and research skills.

---

## 🎯 Goals

- Learn how researchers think and solve problems.
- Develop critical reading skills.
- Build a strong foundation in agricultural remote sensing and AI.
- Create a personal knowledge archive that I can revisit during future research.

---

## 📂 Structure

Each paper has its own folder containing:

- `README.md` — My notes and key takeaways
- `paper.pdf` *(optional)*
- `images/` *(optional)* — Important figures from the paper

---

## 📝 Note Template

Each paper note answers a few simple questions:

- What problem does this paper solve?
- Why is it important?
- What did I learn?
- What methods or datasets were used?
- What are the limitations?
- What questions do I still have?
- How might this help my future research?

---

## 📖 Paper Management

- **Zotero** is used to manage papers, citations, and tags.
- **GitHub** is used only for my learning notes and reflections.

---

> **My goal is to understand research, not to collect papers.**
